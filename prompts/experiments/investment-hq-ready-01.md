## 3. Define the target operating experience

The desired future experience is that the human owner can open BotSquad HQ and assign an investment mandate such as:

"Operate a small, finite paper-investment research program. Review a selected universe, debate investment opportunities, apply risk limits, document HOLD/BUY/SELL recommendations, and track outcomes using approved market evidence."

BotSquad should then be able to:

1. Understand the mandate and its owner-approved limits.
2. Identify the responsible existing employees.
3. Organize appropriate research and discussion.
4. Read permitted, time-qualified market evidence.
5. Produce genuine independent analyses.
6. Review, challenge and revise recommendations.
7. Submit valid paper actions when specifically authorized.
8. Record trusted simulator outcomes.
9. Retain positions, cash, valuation, risk and historical decisions.
10. Revisit earlier conclusions when a future approved operating window arrives.
11. Present execution, cost, uncertainty and performance in HQ.
12. Stop when its finite authorization ends.

This should use the existing worker identities, hierarchy, discussion mechanisms, runtime bindings, dispatcher, investment team, simulator and scheduler whenever possible.

**Do not design a separate hardcoded Atlas/Maya bot pipeline.**

Employees should retain their individual responsibilities and genuine reasoning within explicit authority limits.

The mandate is strategic direction, not implicit permission for arbitrary new model calls, market-data access or financial effects.
