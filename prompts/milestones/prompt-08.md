## Goal

Let persistent specialists reason together in a bounded first-class deliberation with explicit shared evidence.

## Required behavior

- Create a charter, participants, facilitator/synthesizer, bounded turns/time and attributable transcript.
- Support owner interjection, actual response/refinement, alternatives, dissent and versioned synthesis.
- Reuse continuity and explicitly exported evidence; separate a recommendation from owner-submitted follow-on work.

## Authority / safety boundaries

- Drafts and ordinary messages remain passive; explicit start/extension authorizes bounded work.
- Membership does not grant private conversation/history access. Public research needs separate discussion-mode grants.
- Preserve consumed budgets, checkpoints, shared capacity and unknown-provider fences; no hidden Task or automatic assignment.

## Acceptance expectations

- C08-1: real cross-functional deliberation, owner interjection, substantive peer response and inspectable synthesis; separately authorized assignment.
- C08-2: context replacement, recovery/interruption, privacy, no replay, idle and earlier workflow regressions.

## Explicit non-goals

Recurring scheduler, live business actions, Computer Use, unbounded meetings and automatic production grants.

## Historical outcome

Actual supplied-material and public-evidence groups, continuity, independent review and production preservation passed. Failed attempts remain in the validation record.
