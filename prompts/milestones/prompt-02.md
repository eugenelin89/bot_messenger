## Goal

Extend the organization with product specification, two concurrent engineers, independent review and tested integration.

## Required behavior

- Coordinate Atlas, Maya, Turing, Linus, Ada and Grace around the bounded SquadStatus fixture.
- Allocate distinct Task-owned branches/worktrees and narrow source/test/Git tools.
- Persist immutable submissions and exact-commit reviews; advance canonical main only after trusted full tests.

## Authority / safety boundaries

- Preserve Prompt 01 identity, delegation, audit and event-driven dispatch.
- Treat engineer code as untrusted; confine tests and restrict each allocation to its approved source scope.
- Retain conflicts, failures and ambiguous effects; no worker-selected arbitrary paths, native shell, browser or network.

## Acceptance expectations

- Actual overlapping engineer turns and inspectable commits, independent Grace review, passing integrated product tests and manager evaluation.
- Restart without duplicate work; verify denied scope probes, failed integration and retained Prompt 01 behavior.

## Explicit non-goals

General Projects, external repositories/publication, dependency installation, deployment, Computer Use and spending.

## Historical outcome

Accepted real six-worker macOS engineering with overlap, exact review, 8/8 product tests and recovery. Generalized repository lifecycle belongs to Prompt 05.
