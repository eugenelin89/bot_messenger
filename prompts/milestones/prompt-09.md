## Goal

Connect owner mandates, company-selected internal work, attributable decisions, observations and a durable review clock.

## Required behavior

- Keep broad and specific mandates first-class; let the company choose research, groups and bounded internal Tasks.
- Persist source-grounded decisions and mode-labelled observations with actual delivery evidence.
- Schedule bounded one-time/interval/daily reviews with timezone, version, occurrence, cancellation, coalescing and overlap semantics.

## Authority / safety boundaries

- Owner activation and typed immutable bounds authorize work; strategy and timer callbacks add no permissions.
- Preserve hierarchy, private Task contexts, source withdrawal, consumed budgets and uncertainty fences.
- Asymmetri Motion is reference configuration; use approved read-only/sanitized/fixture evidence, never invented business metrics.

## Acceptance expectations

- C09-1–C09-4: actual broad mandate plus two Asymmetri reference cycles, with a real scheduled follow-up and evidence-based next decision.
- Controlled-clock restart/duplicate/edit/pause/revoke/overlap/missed-run tests, context continuity, independent review and no idle work.

## Explicit non-goals

Live product changes, outreach/publication, private analytics, spending, Computer Use and generic automation platform.

## Historical outcome

Real workers and elapsed scheduling passed using explicitly synthetic product evidence. This proved the internal loop, not live business operation. The 30-second schedule-admission floor was added later by stabilization 01; it is not an original Prompt 09 requirement.
