## Goal

Close one real approved business action → receipt → observation → scheduled reassessment loop for a single company.

## Required behavior

- Admit attributable typed business evidence and let employees investigate, deliberate and persist a Decision.
- Keep external intent, exact human approval, executor attempt and provider receipt distinct.
- Use the narrow GitHub document adapter and durable company clock for a second evidence-based cycle.

## Authority / safety boundaries

- One exact document scope per owner grant; service-private credentials never enter worker context.
- Recheck authority/source before transmission; withdrawal and revocation deny further actions; unknown effects fence the canonical target.
- Approval waits release model capacity. Deployment, strategic prose, Computer Use and device v1 create no business authority.

## Acceptance expectations

- C11-1 useful bounded evidence/capability; C11-2 exact approved real effect, receipt and independent read-back.
- C11-3 two scheduled cycles with continuity/recovery and supervision; C11-4 baseline/sources, measured result, unknown costs and justified next decision.
- Fixtures and code deployment alone cannot satisfy the live gate; keep failures, interventions and limitations visible.

## Explicit non-goals

Broad unattended business operation, all connectors, arbitrary GitHub changes, autonomous spending, treasury, multi-company and a numbered follow-on milestone.

## Historical outcome
