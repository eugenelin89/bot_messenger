# BotSquad Prompt Authoring Instructions

These instructions supplement the root `AGENTS.md` for files under `prompts/`.

Before drafting or revising a substantive milestone prompt, read:

1. `docs/PROJECT_MEMORY.md` for the concise continuation handoff;
2. `docs/product/ROADMAP.md` for current sequence/status;
3. Decisions 016 and 017 for accepted company behavior, priorities and authority boundaries;
4. `docs/product/SINGLE_COMPANY_OPERATIONS.md` for detailed operating/continuity acceptance;
5. `prompts/MILESTONE_REQUIREMENTS.md` for the applicable acceptance IDs.

Verify current main and active implementation plans before relying on a status recap.

Do not silently restore the superseded future 11–14 roadmap. Prompt 11 now means practical
single-company business operations; multi-company, company collaboration, generic Telegram
identities and federation are deferred and unnumbered.

Keep Prompt 07 context rollover, Prompt 09 minimal durable scheduling and Asymmetri Motion
reference acceptance, and Prompt 11's approved live-business evidence gate explicit in the
applicable prompt. Do not fold later milestones into the current task or imply that these
features already exist. Preserve broad and specific mandates, meaningful team choices,
scoped memory, bounded model work, independent evidence and trusted human authority.

A bootstrap/maintenance prompt remains bounded to its requested purpose; this guidance is
not permission to make it implement future company features. Do not rewrite historical
prompt/validation records as though they included requirements accepted later.
