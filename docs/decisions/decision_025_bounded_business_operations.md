# Decision 025 — Exact supervised business actions and attributable outcome review

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Status:** Implemented and deployed; separate live acceptance pending
**Extends:** Decisions 017, 019 and 022; preserves 015, 018, 020, 021 and 024

## Decision

Use one small typed GitHub document adapter to connect a mandate's real baseline, current
initiative-linked Decision, exact human approval, trusted receipt, source observation and
scheduled reassessment. Retain evidence, Decision, action, approval, attempt and receipt as
separate durable objects. A model's statement or group consensus cannot substitute for any
trusted boundary. No Asymmetri-specific target or content belongs in the generic engine.

An existing coordinator with current capability and a separately issued exact provider grant
may observe or propose. Other employees may investigate explicitly shared evidence through
existing bounded internal work. No retained worker role/capability profile changes; no hiring
expansion or general business-admin role is needed for this slice. The executor holds the
credential and performs the effect only after exact immutable approval and fresh authority checks.

Approval binds identity, scope, prior bytes/blob, new bytes/hash, all disclosures, policy,
credential reference and expiry. One original worker execution owns one distinct intent. Its
explicit action wait releases the model slot and retains the original cycle deadline. The
independent executor reserves the resource and worker only while preparing/transmitting. A
terminal result wakes one new strategic generation; late receipts outlive the original model.

Record transmission intent before I/O. A possible effect without a receipt becomes unknown,
fences the canonical target across grants/workers, and never blindly retries. Owner read-only
reconciliation may establish a labelled correlated receipt. Compensation is a distinct exact
action and approval, not an automatic rollback or permission inferred from a stop request.

Use a fixed provider host and operation; validate repository/tree/blob identity and keep
protected repository-restricted credentials outside workers and evidence. GitHub blob CAS
does not bind unrelated branch files, prevent ABA or undo automation. The owner must review
repository workflow and downstream effects. This adapter does not create/merge PRs or branches.

Business observations retain source, time/window, metric, baseline, missingness, privacy, mode
and limitations. REAL LIVE, REAL READ ONLY, PUBLIC, OWNER PROVIDED, SANITIZED and SIMULATED
remain distinct. Original delivery and withdrawal rules apply to business sources and derived
actions. A document being present is operational evidence, never proof of growth or revenue.

## Delivery and pilot boundary

Migration 14 is additive and creates zero grants, actions, approvals, receipts or model work.
Software deployment does not activate a pilot. C11-2 needs an separately approved real effect
and provider receipt; C11-3/4 need scheduled actual follow-up and an evidence-based decision.
Fixtures and real-model operation against a fixture establish only their stated scope.

The owner selected the Asymmetri Motion GitHub repository for read-only discovery. That selection
does not authorize writes. The candidate is a small operations-document clarity improvement.
Its normal short-lived branch/review workflow is preferred; a direct-main exception requires
explicit exact owner authorization and cannot bypass provider protections. Private source and
review packets remain protected outside public repository evidence.

Defer more adapters, CRM, spending, releases, customer outreach, App Store and federation.
