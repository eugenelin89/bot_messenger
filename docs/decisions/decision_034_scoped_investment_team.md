# Decision 034 — Scoped investment team and committed public evidence

**Date:**2026-10-10. **Status:** Accepted for reviewed source integration only. Genuine employee acceptance and production activation remain pending.

## Decision

An owner can preview and consent to one fresh owner-selected working group for one frozen synthetic paper run. The exact envelope binds existing participants, reviewed public-candidate names/responsibilities, proposer/reviewer/submitter subsets, expiry, execution/research/proposal/order quotas, per-turn input/output/cost ceilings and the same run's publication channel/roster. No worker is hired, renamed or globally reconfigured by this capability. Draft preview revisions are retained; the current digest and evidence checkpoint must match consent. After consent the envelope is immutable. Migration18 adds empty scopes, previews, execution reservations, immutable attributed proposals/reviews/submissions and controls; it seeds no authority.

Reuse WorkingGroups, Conversations and the shared two-slot dispatcher. The separate `investment-discussion-v1` schema persists even after pause/revocation, so ordinary discussion fallback cannot reacquire execution authority. Claim reserves the full per-turn maximum once. Fresh provider handoffs, group context and runtime identity text use approved experiment responsibilities instead of private missions. Current scope, membership, expiry, source binding and existing uncertainty fences apply on callbacks. Pause/revoke signal the active runtime; scope expiry participates in its abort deadline. Aborting never asserts provider settlement or refunds consumed reservations.

The trusted runtime adapter must implement `runBoundedInvestment` and enforce every supplied token/cost bound before and between provider effects, including abort. There is no fallback to ordinary `run`. The current Codex adapter has usage notifications and time/tool bounds, but no such enforceable usage envelope; actual investment model dispatch remains held. Broker search also remains held until its separate enforceable model-cost adapter exists. Existing public HTTPS research grants/eligibility and aggregate operation quotas remain mandatory; group membership does not grant research.

Typed tools inspect, propose, read, independently review and submit HOLD/BUY/SELL. Worker/execution identity and timestamps come from trusted context. Proposals commit complete narrative, alternatives, risks, invalidation, exact research/contribution hashes, market references and private simulator hash atomically with the journal, public intent and tool receipt. Complete original sources must have been delivered to the same execution, including all pages of long contributions. The reviewer reads the full proposal and its underlying cited sources; author and reviewer must differ, and upstream executions must be settled. One authoritative review per decision revision fits pinned v1; a rejected or changed proposal needs a new revision. A newer revision supersedes the old one. HOLD creates no order and consumes no order allowance. Semantic retries retain original timestamps and exact receipts.

BUY submission and fill pass the serialized market-aware facade and recheck current publication health, roster, rights, generation and consent. Uncaptured/new narrative evidence therefore blocks new risk until exact owner publication approval; future-financial capture alone cannot authorize new prose. SELL can reduce risk without healthy publication, while ordinary frozen simulator, source-use and team authority remain enforced. Exact committed/terminal replay is observational and retains its receipt. Team capability controls differ from the future operating loop's run pause/cancel controls.

`CommittedTeamPublicationSource` reads actual persisted contributions, original evidence, synthesis, proposals and independent reviews; it never populates `FixtureDecisionCopy` with employee prose. Publication requires recorded settled execution provenance, exact hashes, current group sharing/roster and separately selected excerpt rights. A fixture runtime is explicitly labelled and cannot be presented as an actual employee. Existing synthetic accounting/market mode remains unchanged. Private original IDs map to opaque public identities. Contiguous public contribution ordinals exclude charter/notes/synthesis summaries; those references map to the real discussion or exact safe artifact version. Draft/final artifacts preserve per-author versions and predecessor links when the finalizer changes. Structured private participation/provider details stay out of the derivative. Unlicensed excerpts have a safe withheld registry record. Public research links are metadata, never evidence of a copying license.

All material still passes the independent exact publisher preview/consent. New prose/artifacts never become automatically public. Current source checks stop staged bytes after sharing withdrawal. A finalized investment group cannot be extended or finished again, including after stop; pinned v1 permits one public discussion closure. One immutable team scope per run is an intentional source boundary, not a generic reusable investment organization platform.
