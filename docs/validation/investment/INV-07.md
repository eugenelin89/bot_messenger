## Implemented path

Owner-local `/investment-publication` shows configured source/destination choices, exact canonical records and normalized artifact bytes (including rendered PNG), explicit audience/participants/classes/formats, request/byte/backlog limits and expiry. No source or transport is configured by ordinary startup. Navigation/preview starts no runtime, model or delivery. Input edits, failed replacements and late/out-of-order responses invalidate prior approval. Exact consent is replay-safe and records a private immutable receipt.

Schema17 creates no authority. Existing worker/task/group/message/grant/schedule/operation state and the disabled private simulator outbox remain intact. The journal trigger captures private intent atomically with accounting. Public identity/sequence mappings, preview and derivative hashes, queued bytes, attempts, results and highwaters are retained. Public financial ledger hashes are distinct from private journal hashes. Acknowledgements are storage receipts, never simulator commands.

A default finite snapshot grant covers its preview. An explicit `futureFinancial` opt-in permits one-step owner capture under the standing envelope; it is not a timer or daemon. Captured financial/order/status changes depend on approved decision content. Newly introduced prose, artifacts and standalone/nested action-source descriptions cannot use this exemption. New originals/versions are independently hash-checked. Current run/participant binding and all required market uses remain enforced. Uncaptured journal records count toward health and expose `newRiskBlocked`; INV-08/09 must consume this signal at admission before claiming an enforced investment risk stop. It is not connected to the standalone simulator in this packet; bounded catch-up delivery remains possible under active consent after the age threshold.

Transport has fixed canonical paths/authority, normal HTTPS verification, fresh RFC9421 Ed25519 signatures, bounded body/response/deadline and no redirects. Literal HTTP127.0.0.1 is fixture-only. Requests, total transmitted bytes (receipt GET costs zero), backlog and expiry are finite. Known permanent errors block; outages use increasing bounded jittered backoff and bounded429 delay. Lost outcomes retain independent reconciliation state through auth errors, rotation and replacement consent. Lease replacement cannot erase a valid late receipt or downgrade delivered state. Same-byte content PUT refreshes expired unpublished receiver staging.

Restart requires local/receiver generation advancement, new exact consent and two remote proofs: authenticated batch receipt/absence plus current receiver status/absence. Empty receivers and absent final attempts recover without replaying uncertain writes blindly. Receiver-ahead backups remain blocked. Durable generation and process-instance claims prevent older instances and overlapping losing restorers from adopting another instance's authority. Current authority is rechecked immediately before each send and after async restoration reads.
