normal team scopes, working groups, scheduler, dispatcher and finite runtime accounting. They
inspect actual retrieval/provenance/quality and unknown historical availability, contribute
under their own identities, review the full draft independently and revise a private report.

retention deadlines, and records separate collection and AI-processing consent. Preview and
saved scope create no service authority, HTTP request or model invocation.

The authenticated owner collection action selects only a frozen request hash and exact scope
digest. Trusted custody consumes its independently retained allowance before transmission,
then uses the existing FinancialDataGateway, controlled direct HTTPS transport and pinned
Python/yfinance parser. Raw response bodies remain memory-only. The normalized projection
retains original observation/revision/predecessor identities and hashes. Every citing worker
must receive all evidence pages in its current execution. Research has no simulator portfolio,
paper-price qualification, employee shell/HTTP, public channel or Task export.

Schema24 preserves retained paper identities and reparents three attribution foreign keys
through an immutable typed run registry. New research tables are empty on migration. Trusted
retention redacts local provider-derived content and mirrors while retaining attribution,
approvals, hashes and consumption. A purge receipt requires successful WAL truncation and,
for observed or host-verified runs, independently joined runtime deletion.
