## What counts as an outcome

Typed **HOLD** is an action with proposal, independent approval and submission, no order/fill. **Rejection** is an independent review disposition and must retain its reason; it can be a legitimate no-trade outcome. **Submission denial** is distinct from review rejection and from an admitted order. **No submitted decision** describes today's discussion; it must not be relabeled formal HOLD. `NO-TRADE` is not a separate action enum in the reviewed tools. The public record should use supported factual categories.

A new BUY/SELL requires scoped instrument/quantity, risk and cash/position checks, exact settled independent review, cutoff compliance, source qualification, price guard, immutable order identity, modeled fill and accounting. An approved order can later expire or reject for price/data reasons without being a duplicate execution or an employee research failure. No trade is required merely to demonstrate activity.
