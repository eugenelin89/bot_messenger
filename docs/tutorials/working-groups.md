# Learn by doing: ask your team to reason together

This tutorial describes the Prompt 08 UI. Check the
[acceptance report](../validation/PROMPT_08_VALIDATION.md) for delivery status.
Use the trusted private browser connection to your HQ. Choose a hypothetical internal
question while learning; a discussion gives employees no implementation authority.

1. Open **Working Groups → New working group**. Enter a problem such as “Compare guided
   setup, an interactive example and contextual help for a small offline tool.” Specify
   the desired recommendation and constraints: one engineer-week, privacy, accessibility,
   no implementation or publication.
2. Select two to six existing eligible employees, a facilitator and synthesizer, or
   explicitly check **Let Atlas organize this discussion**. Atlas selects from the shown
   eligible roster; it does not hire. Choose whether an explicitly incomplete result is
   acceptable if a participant fails. Create the draft; this invokes no model.
3. Review the charter, limits and audience. Use **Share selected material** for a brief
   or an exact selected document/public-source excerpt. Confirm the displayed audience.
   Only that export enters the group; private conversations and full company documents
   stay outside it. During Atlas's selection, review the entire possible audience.
4. Click **Start bounded discussion**. If HQ dispatch is globally paused, resume it only
   when you intend its queued work to run. The group progresses through real employee
   contributions, targeted responses and a reviewed synthesis without owner speaker
   selection on every turn.
5. Add a question or constraint under **Owner interjection**. Subsequent authorized turns
   receive its ordered revision. Running contributions show the earlier checkpoint they
   actually saw. A note after completion remains passive until a separate extra round.
6. Inspect **Shared evidence packet**, contribution references and **Scheduled work and
   continuity**. A source excerpt preserves origin/time/hash/omission details; a public
   analogy is not proof of your product's user behavior.
7. Read **Saved synthesis → Read synthesis and unresolved concerns**, or download its
   JSON. Read the alternatives, evidence limits, dissent and unresolved questions as well
   as the recommendation. It is not an owner decision or implementation approval.
8. If useful, click **Prepare separate assignment**. Review the selected synthesis and
   edit the objective, constraints and acceptance criteria. Only **Submit assignment to
   Atlas** creates a normal Task. Start with a harmless internal assessment.

Research is optional. A group works from supplied material with no grants. For public
research, open an eligible worker's **Capabilities & research**, explicitly enable Public
Research with the working-group checkbox, and permit research in the group charter.
Existing Task/conversation grants do not expand automatically. To extend an existing
grant, revoke it and explicitly grant again with that option. Engineers/reviewers do not
gain lookup authority merely by joining. Authorized researchers can share bounded group
sources, and peers can read those exports without research permission.

Use **Pause future turns** to hold the group while active work finishes. Use **Interrupt
active group work** separately to request cancellation. **Stop discussion** starts no
summarizer. **Finish with current evidence** explicitly authorizes one final synthesis
once active work settles. **Authorize another bounded round** adds eight turns, one round
and thirty minutes, at most twice; consumed work remains recorded. Pauses do not stop the
sixty-minute wall clock. Resume never overrides global pause or provider uncertainty.

An unknown provider outcome requires inspection and remains fenced. Revoking a member or
withdrawing evidence blocks future delivery; already delivered context cannot be recalled.
Create a new group with a reviewed packet to continue under a different sharing scope.
Archive settled groups to keep them read-only. Recurring meetings, automatic implementation
and Computer Use are outside this milestone.

For a blocked group whose original charter permits incomplete results, the owner can
select another current, unfenced participant when choosing **Finish with current evidence**.
This queues one bounded final synthesis and records the previous and selected authors.
It does not alter membership, grant permissions, or clear the original worker fence.
The resulting artifact lists failed participation; prior artifacts stay immutable.
