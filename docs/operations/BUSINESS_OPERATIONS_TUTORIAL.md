# Supervised business document operation

Prompt 11 does not give BotSquad unrestricted authority over the business. Use the trusted
local browser over the existing SSH tunnel. Paired Client API v1 devices cannot manage or
approve this operation. The [validation record](../validation/PROMPT_11_VALIDATION.md) states
whether the real pilot is complete; fixture demonstrations are visibly SIMULATED.

## Prepare a source without activating work

1. Review an existing owner-controlled repository, its branch workflow and automation. Choose
   one existing branch and ordinary Markdown document. This adapter does not create branches,
   PRs, releases, accounts, app binaries or deployments. Prefer the repository's normal review
   workflow. Never infer permission for direct-main edits from a general business mandate.
2. Have the service operator provision a credential restricted to that numeric repository ID
   with Contents read/write and required metadata read access. Do not paste it into BotSquad
   messages, worker files, screenshots or public evidence. Existing broad credentials are not
   an acceptable substitute. Verify the actual selected-repository scope in provider settings.
3. Store the credential and configuration in protected root/service-owned directories, private
   mode-0600 regular files, without symlink ancestors. Configure only the service environment:
   `BOTSQUAD_BUSINESS_CONFIG=/etc/botsquad/business/providers.json`. An illustrative configuration
   contains fake identifiers, never a real secret:

   ```json
   [{"ref":"docs-pilot","token_file":"/etc/botsquad/business/github-token","repository_id":12345,"repository_restricted":true,"targets":[{"repository":"example/operations","repository_id":12345,"branch":"docs/pilot","path":"README.md"}]}]
   ```

   Restart through normal operator maintenance after checking active work. Configuration alone
   creates no business grant, mandate, approval or model invocation. The adapter fails closed
   when the file, credential, target, provider or repository restriction is unavailable.
4. Create a passive mandate with an existing coordinator. Define the objective, success/stop
   criteria, no-spending/privacy constraints, evidence modes, cycle deadlines and execution/
   scheduling bounds. Include REAL READ ONLY and REAL LIVE for this live source, or only the
   explicitly intended fixture modes during isolated acceptance. Do not create a new role.
5. In **Business operations → Authorize one document scope**, enter the exact repository ID,
   slug, existing branch/path, opaque configured credential reference, expiry within 24 hours
   and 1–3 proposal allowance. This permits bounded reads/proposals; it approves no effect.
6. Select **Read source / define baseline**. Define the outcome, source/window/unit/denominator,
   freshness, missing fields, privacy, expected lag and attribution limits. The adapter records
   exact provider content/hashes with visible mode. Record missing customer analytics and costs
   as unknown. Failed retrievals do not fabricate measurements.

## Let the company investigate and propose

Activate the mandate only after the internal scope is approved. The coordinator chooses useful
internal investigation, participants and alternatives. Working groups are optional; a trivial
documentation correction may not need one. Research grants remain separate and private business
material is not authorized for public search. Selected Task/group exports are explicit.

Inspect the baseline, initiative and current Decision. The worker may propose one unique text
replacement against its fully read baseline. It cannot approve itself, pick another provider,
change credentials or perform raw network writes. The explicit approval wait releases its model
slot. Check the cycle deadline: an expired or stopped cycle cannot execute an old approval.

## Review the exact effect

Open **Review exact action and receipt**. Review the provider/adapter/mode, worker, mandate/cycle,
Decision and alternatives; repository ID/slug/branch/path; unique old/new text and complete
documents; expected blob and baseline head; new blob/content SHA-256; full commit message;
immutable intent hash, policy and expiry; measurement, privacy, risks, cost and compensation.

Confirm which real system changes. Account for CI, webhooks, notifications and repository rules.
The credential mechanism is service-only. Provider incremental fees may be zero if verified;
unknown costs must stay unknown. A branch commit alone does not prove corrected `main` content
or deployed website state. Normal human integration is a recorded intervention when required.

Choose **Approve exact action** only after checking the review acknowledgment, or **Deny**.
The backend binds that decision to the displayed immutable hash and revalidates authority,
source state and worker settlement before transmission. Approval cannot be widened by text or
a later edit. The adapter does not bypass branch protection or create a replacement account.

## Inspect outcomes and the scheduled review

The receipt shows the actual commit/blob/parent and request metadata. An action is not confirmed
until a trusted receipt is retained. Inspect the follow-up trusted document observation separately.
An equal blob proves presence at that read time; it establishes no adoption, revenue or reader
benefit. Real customer signals require separately authorized evidence and appropriate lag.

The coordinator should persist a Prompt 09 review and close Cycle 1. Inspect its due time,
occurrence and bounded count. Let the company clock initiate Cycle 2; do not manually prompt
each internal handoff. Cycle 2 should read the prior Decision, receipt, actual observation,
usage/cost information and interventions, then explain a continue/iterate/pivot/stop decision.
A later review may be appropriate when business impact cannot yet be measured. Model usage is
available in execution events where supplied; monetary charges remain unknown unless sourced.

## Stop, revoke and uncertainty

- Pause/stop the mandate or cancel its future review through the existing controls.
- Revoke the provider grant to deny new access/effects, or cancel/revoke an individual pending
  action. A transmitted effect may still finish; its late receipt and intervention remain.
- If outcome is unknown, the target is fenced. **Request read-only reconciliation** reads
  bounded provider history and never resends. A matching history receipt is labelled correlated
  evidence; it does not cryptographically identify the writer. Inconclusive results stay unknown.
- **Request compensation proposal** records a passive request for the next authorized company
  review. It does not dispatch a model, create a grant or undo anything. A restoration needs a
  fresh baseline/Decision and exact new approval. History and downstream effects remain.
- Withdraw evidence to deny future source and derivative delivery. This retains owner history
  and cannot retract content already received by a model/provider. It blocks stale decisions
  and untransmitted actions; it never deletes an existing receipt or uncertainty fence.

If credentials, branch setup or exact approval are unavailable, leave the live gate pending.
Do not replace it with a fixture claim or broaden scope. See the
[technical limits](../architecture/BUSINESS_OPERATIONS.md) before expanding a pilot.
